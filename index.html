Filing Tip: Combined Records

Since you group your vendor and core products together:

  • Gross Sales: Ensure your daily cash-up or Z-report includes the total money taken from both waffles/coffee and the vendor food.
  • Vendor Payments: Even if you don't track the *selling* price separately, keep all invoices/receipts from the vendors as proof of your **Purchases** to justify the lower profit margin.
  • Entertainment: Remember that staff treats are 100% deductible, while dinners with stakeholders/vendors are limited to 50%.
